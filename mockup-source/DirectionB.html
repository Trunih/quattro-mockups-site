Class-average pricing can't see your building.

Most long-term care liability is priced off state and facility-type averages, re-rated once a year, with no way to reward a well-run facility, and hard caps on the coverage that matters most when a claim gets serious.

Typical market sublimits on assault and battery, sexual abuse and molestation, and wandering and elopement claims are often inclusive of defense costs. A single serious claim can burn through the entire sublimit in legal fees before it ever reaches the injured party.

1A&B

Frequently sublimited far below the underlying policy limit.

2SAM

Often the most restricted line on the policy, despite rising exposure.

3W&E

Common in memory care, treated as a footnote instead of a core exposure.

4Rating

Happens once a year, off static data, with no way to reflect today.

Two signals already inside your building.

We don't ask operators to install anything new. At submission, Quattro's proprietary AI reads 90 days of the clinical and monitoring data already generated in the facility, alongside the application itself, to underwrite at the facility level instead of a state or class average.

1

Clinical signal: what your care records already show

Incident documentation, care planning, staffing patterns and clinical outcomes, read over the 90 days preceding your application.

2

Observed signal: what's happening on the floor, today

Fall response times, supervision patterns and safety events from monitoring already installed, over that same 90-day window.

Quattro's proprietary AI combines both signals into a single, facility-level risk profile used to underwrite the policy. Once bound, we keep watching both signals and send early-warning alerts to help prevent incidents, with that same data informing underwriting again at renewal.

Zero new hardware to install. This adds nothing to your clinical or facilities staff's workload; we work with what's already running in the building.
