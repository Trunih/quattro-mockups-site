Class-average pricing can't see your building.

Most long-term care liability is still priced off state and facility-type averages, then re-rated once a year. It has no way to reward a facility that is actually run well, and it caps the coverage that matters most right when a claim gets serious.

Typical sublimits are often inclusive of defense costs. A single serious claim can burn through the entire sublimit in legal fees before it ever reaches the injured party.

A&B

Assault and battery is frequently sublimited far below the underlying policy limit.

SAM

Sexual abuse and molestation coverage is often the most restricted line on the policy.

W&E

Wandering and elopement claims are treated as a footnote instead of a core exposure.

Rating

Underwriting happens once a year, off static data, not what's happening today.

Two signals already inside your building.

We don't ask operators to install anything new. At submission, Quattro's proprietary AI reads 90 days of the clinical and monitoring data already generated in the facility, alongside the application itself, to underwrite at the facility level.

What your care records already show

Clinical signal

Incident documentation, care planning activity, staffing patterns, and clinical outcomes, reviewed over the 90 days preceding your application.

What's actually happening on the floor

Observed signal

Fall response times, supervision patterns, and safety-relevant events from monitoring already installed, over that same 90-day window.

Once bound, we keep watching both signals and send early-warning alerts to help prevent incidents, with that data informing underwriting again at renewal. No new hardware, no added burden on staff.
