Class-average pricing can't see your building.

Most long-term care liability is still priced off state and facility-type averages, then re-rated once a year. It has no way to reward a facility that is actually run well, and it caps the coverage that matters most right when a claim gets serious.

$25,000–$100,000 typical sublimit → Quattro: full limits, no sublimit

Typical market sublimits on assault and battery, sexual abuse and molestation, and wandering and elopement claims, often inclusive of defense costs. A single serious claim can burn through the entire sublimit in legal fees before it ever reaches the injured party.

A&B

Assault and battery is frequently sublimited far below the underlying policy limit.

SAM

Sexual abuse and molestation coverage is often the most restricted line on the policy, even though exposure is rising.

W&E

Wandering and elopement claims, common in memory care, are treated as a footnote instead of a core exposure.

Rating

Underwriting happens once a year, off static data, with no way to reflect what is actually happening in the building today.

Two signals already inside your building, read at the facility level.

We don't ask operators to install anything new. At submission, Quattro's proprietary AI reads 90 days of the clinical and monitoring data already generated in your facility, alongside the application itself, to underwrite at the facility level instead of a state or class average.

Clinical signal

What your care records already show

Incident documentation, care planning activity, staffing patterns, and clinical outcomes from the systems your clinical teams already use every day, reviewed over the 90 days preceding your application.

Observed signal

What's actually happening on the floor

Fall response times, supervision patterns, and safety-relevant events from monitoring systems already installed in your facility, reviewed over that same 90-day window rather than a single self-reported snapshot.

Quattro's proprietary AI combines both signals into a single, facility-level risk profile used to underwrite your policy. Once bound, we keep watching both signals and send your team early-warning alerts to help prevent incidents before they happen, with that same ongoing data informing underwriting again at renewal.

No new hardware. No added burden on your clinical or facilities staff. We work with what's already running in the building.
