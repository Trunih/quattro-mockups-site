Two signals already present within your facility

We don't ask operators to install anything new. Our AI reads ninety days of clinical and monitoring data already generated in the facility, alongside the application itself, to underwrite at the facility level.

Clinical signal

Incident documentation, care planning activity, staffing patterns, and clinical outcomes, reviewed over the ninety days preceding your application.

Observed signal

Fall response times, supervision patterns, and safety-relevant events from monitoring already installed, over that same window.

Once bound, we keep watching both signals and send early-warning alerts, with that same data informing underwriting again at renewal. No new hardware, no added burden on staff.
